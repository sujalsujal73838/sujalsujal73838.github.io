Path Quest – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 November 2026 · App: Path Quest (Android, package com.pathquest.game) · Developer:Sujal M, India

This policy explains what information Path Quest (“the game”) handles, why, and the choices you have. In short: the game itself keeps your progress only on your device, and it shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device information to show and measure those ads.

1. Information the game keeps on your device

To save your progress, the game stores the following in its private storage on your phone:

  • levels unlocked, stars, best times, collectibles found and daily-challenge streaks;
  • gameplay statistics (for example moves made and hints used);
  • your settings (sound, music, graphics and controls);
  • the explorer name and avatar you choose in the game (no real name is needed);
  • a counter used to space out ads between levels.

This information never leaves your device to us. We do not run servers for the game, we have no user accounts, and we do not use analytics or crash-reporting services. If you have Android’s device backup turned on, Android may include the game’s storage in your backup to your Google Account; that backup is controlled by you and Google.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads using the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob): a short ad between some levels, and optional “rewarded” ads you can choose to watch for a revive or an extra hint. To show, measure and protect these ads, Google may collect and process:

InformationUsed for
Device and advertising identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID)Advertising, ad measurement, fraud prevention, frequency limits
Approximate location (derived from your IP address)Showing ads relevant to your region, fraud prevention
App interactions (ads shown, ads tapped, ad completion)Advertising, measurement, rewards
Diagnostics (crash logs and performance of the ad software)Keeping the ad software working
Device information (model, operating system, language, network)Delivering ads that work on your device

This information is collected and processed by Google, not by us. Google uses it as described in its own policies:

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Ads are limited to content rated suitable for general and parental-guidance audiences. Information is sent over encrypted connections.

3. Your consent and choices

  • Consent. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent through Google’s consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & ad choices.
  • Advertising ID. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on your phone).
  • Rewarded ads are optional. You never have to watch one to keep playing.
  • Playing offline. The game works fully without an internet connection. Ads and the consent message need a connection, so none are shown while you are offline.

4. Permissions

The game asks for internet access only, which is used for ads and the consent message. The Google Mobile Ads SDK also declares permission to read network state and to use the advertising ID. The game does not access your location (GPS), contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

5. Deleting your data

  • Use Settings → Reset progress in the game, or uninstall the game, to delete everything it stored on your device.
  • We hold no copy of your data, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side.
  • For data processed by Google for advertising, use the advertising-ID controls above and Google’s tools described in its privacy policy.

6. Children

Path Quest is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help.

7. Changes to this policy

If we change how the game handles information, we will update this policy and its effective date. The current version is always available inside the game (Settings → Privacy policy) and on our website.
